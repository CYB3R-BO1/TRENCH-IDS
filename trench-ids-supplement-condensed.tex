\section{Benchmark Overview}
Sequential T1$\to$T6 continual learning for network intrusion detection (3 NetFlow v2 datasets, 10 attack classes, 6 tasks). Retention mechanism: \textbf{experience replay} (200 graphs/task, 30\% fraction); plus a per-class/per-relation memory bank for transferability analysis. All values from versioned artifacts under \texttt{runs/}, \texttt{data/}.

\section{Steps 1--3 Outputs}
Step 1 (quota 390K attack + 130K benign/task, 70/15/15, seed 42): T1 519,999; T2 519,989; T3 519,439 (DDoS 273,636 / Infil 115,804); T4 519,995 (195K/195K); T5 519,999 (Pass 246,903 / Bot 143,097); T6 519,997 (XSS 269,088 / BF 120,912); total \textbf{3,119,418} (verified in \texttt{data/processed/}). Task-size ratio 1.0$\times$.

Step 2 (capture-order chunks $\le$300 flows, 11 relations, benign\_ratio 3.0): train 7,282 + val 1,560 + test 1,560 = \textbf{10,402} graphs (\texttt{data/graphs/graph\_counts.json}; per task 1,214/260/260, T3-train 1,212). Mean flows/graph 299.8; edges/graph $\sim$3,048 (11-relation schema).

Step 3 (\texttt{num\_layers=3}, \texttt{use\_residual=true}, attention fusion, exact ports 0--1023 + 32 tail buckets): 622,482 params total (encoders 86,034 + 3$\times$136,576 relation-conv + 3$\times$42,240 fusion; vocab PROTOCOL 5 / L7 239). 1-layer nominal is 264,850 with 156,562 gradient-reachable (40.9\% unreachable); all 3-layer params are reachable.

Training config (\texttt{configs/train.yaml}): hidden 64, attn 128, lr 0.001, 5 epochs/task, 1 warmup epoch, batch 8, seeds \{42,1,2\}, replay 200/0.3/uniform, EWC/TRD off.

\section{Ablations and Methods}
Architecture ladder (+ replay, 3 seeds; reachable params, current vocab): Flat 0.209/0.739 (265,058); Flat+Host 0.105/0.872 (264,914); Flat+Host matched 0.145/0.832 (156,577); GNN-1L attn 0.174/0.774 (156,562); GNN-1L concat 0.251/0.691; GNN-2L residual 0.115/0.851 (443,666); \textbf{GNN-3L residual 0.081/0.902 (622,482)}. Depth/capacity study, not parameter-matched.

1-layer CL methods: fine-tune 0.693/0.364; EWC 0.669/0.378 (null; 97.8\% Fisher mass in shared params); TRD-uniform 0.651/0.390, transfer 0.647/0.394, \textbf{inverse 0.630/0.411} (meaningful vs.\ uniform), drift 0.689/0.363 (null); \textbf{replay 0.202/0.764}; replay+TRD worse (0.211/0.731).

Forward transfer (B1 LOTO, 3-epoch next-task fine-tune, mean AULC): T1$\to$T2 0.288, T2$\to$T3 0.403, T3$\to$T4 0.187, T4$\to$T5 0.053, T5$\to$T6 0.080 (mean 0.202). Counterfactual relation-reset: \texttt{targeted\_by} positive on all 5 transitions both seeds (s42 mean +0.0116, s1 +0.0082); static predictors (cosine/drift/gradient) do not replicate across seeds.

Cross-dataset: Dataset A accuracy \num{0.0008} (BoT DDoS$\to$Recon 99.3\%); Dataset B collapses to 1--2 known classes (median softmax $\ge$0.89). Replay budget: GNN 25 / Flat+Host 100 selected on val (canonical 3-layer runs use 200). TRD $\lambda_d=0.25$ on val. Joint reference (s42): 0.876 acc / 0.842 macro-F1 (different schedule; not an upper bound).

\section{Reproducibility}
Seeds 42/1/2 (\texttt{seed\_everything}); PyTorch 2.14.0+cu130, RTX 3050. \texttt{python -m trench\_ids.cl.train model.num\_layers=3 model.use\_residual=true replay.enabled=true ewc.lambda\_r=0 ewc.lambda\_s=0 ewc.lambda\_u=0 train.warmup\_epochs=1 train.seed=42 paths.out\_dir=runs/gnn\_3layer\_residual\_s42} (repeat s1/s2). Metrics in \texttt{runs/\textless name\textgreater/summary.json} + \texttt{forgetting\_matrix.json}. Headline: replay cuts forgetting to \textbf{0.081 $\pm$ 0.008}; EWC/TRD variants, TCTRL/RFR/FACT, and cross-dataset generalization are documented negative results.
