## Operation approval template

For each operation, discuss and record only what is needed:

- ID and objective
- Inputs and assumptions
- Algorithm or mathematical procedure, alternatives and rationale
- Expected output and manually worked example
- Verification, failure handling and resource implications
- Approval status and, after implementation, verification evidence / commit
